Connect your Google Sheet

Records are kept in a Google Sheet in your own Google account, so you can open the tracker on any phone or laptop, and clients can open their own link.

How to make the Google Sheet (one time, about 10 minutes)
  1. Sign in to Google, open sheets.new and name the sheet PT Tracker.
  2. Tap . Then in the sheet click Extensions → Apps Script, delete what's there, paste, and click 💾 Save.
  3. At the top, choose setup and click ▶ Run. Allow access when Google asks (click Advanced → Go to project).
  4. Copy the trainer key shown in the Execution log at the bottom.
  5. Click Deploy → New deployment → ⚙ Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy and copy the Web app URL.
  6. Paste both below and tap Connect.

Just try it first

Use the tracker on this device only, with no Google Sheet. Records stay in this browser. Client links won't work until you connect a Google Sheet.
